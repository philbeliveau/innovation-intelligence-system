## Story

**As a** innovation team researcher,
**I want** the pipeline to match consumer insights to innovation techniques and generate directional concepts,
**so that** I can produce actionable initiative ideas without hallucinated financials or unverified claims

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Stage 3 - Technique Matching**: Selects appropriate innovation patterns from SIT/TRIZ/Doblin libraries
2. **Technique Libraries**: Curated JSON files with 5 SIT techniques, 40 TRIZ principles, 10 Doblin types
3. **Defensibility Scoring**: Ranks technique matches by defensibility and applicability
4. **Stage 4 - Concept Generation**: Creates 3-5 directional concepts WITHOUT financial projections
5. **No-Hallucination Boundaries**: Enforces constraints preventing unverified claims
6. **Boundary Documentation**: Every Stage 4 output includes explicit "What we know vs What we infer" section
7. **Integration**: Stages 3-4 successfully receive inputs from Stage 2 and output to Stage 5
8. **Performance**: Stages 3-4 complete in under 3 minutes combined
9. **Testing**: Full test coverage for technique matching and boundary enforcement
